1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The OpenHack platform is a complete, production-grade, self-hostable, and 100% 
offline-capable Hackathon Management & Judging Platform. It covers the full
hackathon lifecycle:
  * Organizer creates hackathon & custom weighted rubrics
  * Participants register for event
  * Participants form teams and invite teammates via secure invite codes
  * Teams submit projects with rich markdown, demo URLs, and GitHub repos
  * Automated round-robin judge assignment balances workload and prevents conflicts
  * Judges evaluate projects across weighted rubric criteria
  * Statistical Z-Score Normalization neutralizes harsh vs. lenient judge bias
  * Public project showcase displays submissions with community upvoting
  * Final results and audit records can be exported in 1 click (CSV & JSON)

The application starts with a single command:
  docker compose up
